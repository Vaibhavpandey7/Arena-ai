# DIL-V1 Insurance Intelligence Studio

Interactive demo for [Va1bhavdev/dil-v1-insurance-model](https://huggingface.co/Va1bhavdev/dil-v1-insurance-model).

### Capabilities
- **Policy Coverage & Exclusions**: Clause interpretation and claim alignment.
- **Actuarial Calculations**: Pure premiums, loss ratios, combined ratios.
- **Solvency II Compliance**: SCR and MCR calculation and capital adequacy evaluation.
- **Regulatory Routing**: FCA Consumer Duty, vulnerable customer handling, and human escalation.

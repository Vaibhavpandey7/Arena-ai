# Insurance Benchmark Official Research Papers

This directory contains the complete academic research papers for all four insurance LLM / LVLM benchmarks evaluated in the Digital Insurance Intelligence Studio.

---

## 1. INS-MMBench
- **File**: [`INS-MMBench_arXiv-2406.09105.pdf`](./INS-MMBench_arXiv-2406.09105.pdf)
- **Title**: *INS-MMBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating LVLMs' Performance in Insurance*
- **Authors**: Chenwei Lin, Hanjia Lyu, Xian Xu, Jiebo Luo (Fudan University & University of Rochester)
- **Venue**: ICCV 2025 · FDU-INS Official Evaluation Suite
- **arXiv**: [arXiv:2406.09105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09105)
- **Scope**: 12,052 Images · 10,372 Questions · 4 Core Verticals (Auto, Household/Commercial Property, Health, Agricultural)
- **Length**: 12 pages

---

## 2. CUFE (CUFEInse v2.0)
- **File**: [`CUFEInse_arXiv-2511.07794.pdf`](./CUFEInse_arXiv-2511.07794.pdf)
- **Title**: *Design, Results and Industry Implications of the World's First Insurance Large Language Model Evaluation Benchmark*
- **Authors**: Hua Zhou, Bing Ma, et al. (School of Insurance & China Institute for Actuarial Science, Central University of Finance and Economics)
- **Venue**: Central University of Finance and Economics (CUFE) Official Benchmark Release
- **arXiv**: [arXiv:2511.07794](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07794)
- **Scope**: 17,637 Professional Questions · 5 Core Pillars · 54 Sub-Indicators · 17 Evaluated LLMs
- **Length**: 16 pages

---

## 3. InsuranceQA
- **File**: [`InsuranceQA_arXiv-1508.01585.pdf`](./InsuranceQA_arXiv-1508.01585.pdf)
- **Title**: *Applying Deep Learning to Answer Selection: A Study and An Open Task*
- **Authors**: Minwei Feng, Bing Xiang, Michael R. Glass, Lidan Wang, Bowen Zhou (IBM Watson / ACL)
- **Venue**: ACL · Insurance Library Real-World QA Corpus
- **arXiv**: [arXiv:1508.01585](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.01585)
- **Scope**: 25,000+ Real Insurance QA Pairs · 500 Candidate Answers per Question (Test-1 & Test-2)
- **Length**: 8 pages

---

## 4. INS-ActBench
- **File**: [`INS-ActBench_arXiv-2607.24273.pdf`](./INS-ActBench_arXiv-2607.24273.pdf)
- **Title**: *INS-ActBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Assessing Professional Actuarial Capability of Large Language Models*
- **Authors**: Changyu Chen, Chenwei Lin, Xian Xu (FDU-INS / Fudan University)
- **Venue**: FDU-INS Official Actuarial Suite
- **arXiv**: [arXiv:2607.24273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24273)
- **Scope**: 12,050 Actuarial Tasks · 16 Global Actuarial Bodies (SOA, CAS, IFoA, DAV) · 3 Core Subsets (Knowledge, Case Analysis, Tool Practice)
- **Length**: 18 pages
